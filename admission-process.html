Admission Process

One step at a time.

Four simple steps, from your first call to your child’s first day at SKVM.

Talk to us

We welcome children from Play Group to Class 5.

Call
+91 94489 15287
Email
principaldisneylandschool@gmail.com
Office hours
Mon to Fri, 9 am to 5 pm
Sat, 9 am to 1 pm
  1. 01

    Call us

    Ring the office or send an email. Tell us your child’s age and the grade you have in mind, and ask anything you like.

  2. 02

    Visit the school

    Come and see the campus near NTTF, Hubli Road. Walk through the classrooms and see where your child will learn.

  3. 03

    Apply

    Pick up the admission form from the office. We will tell you which documents to bring with it.

  4. 04

    Join SKVM

    Once your child’s place is confirmed, the office will let you know when to start.

When you call

Bring your questions.

  • Is there a place in my child’s grade?
  • What are the fees, and when are they due?
  • Which documents should I bring?
  • When does the new school year begin?

We would love to meet you.

Call us or drop in, Monday to Saturday.
