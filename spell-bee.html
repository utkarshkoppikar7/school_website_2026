Spell Bee Competition

Big confidence, one word at a time.

A celebration of language, attentive listening and the courage to speak. An opportunity to discover the stories that words can open.

The power of words

Listen.
Think.
Spell.

Every attempt is a chance to learn something new.

Learning focus

More than remembering a spelling.

01

Listen carefully

Notice sounds, pronunciation and the way a word is used. Careful listening helps build a stronger connection with language.

02

Understand words

Explore meanings and patterns rather than relying only on memorisation. Reading can make vocabulary more familiar.

03

Speak with courage

Practise responding clearly in a supportive setting, and learn to value effort whether an answer is correct or not.

Gentle ways to prepare

Let curiosity lead the practice.

Read a little each day. Keep a notebook of unfamiliar words and explore what they mean. Try saying a word aloud, breaking it into parts and using it in a sentence. Short, encouraging practice can make learning feel more natural.
