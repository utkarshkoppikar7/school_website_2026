Dharwad • Established 1986

A school with deep roots and an enduring purpose.

Sheela Koppikar Vidya Mandir is one of Dharwad’s oldest schools. Established in 1986, its legacy spans more than four decades of being part of the city’s educational life.

“Building leaders with heart & soul” is the promise in our school identity. It began with our founder, Mrs. Sheela Koppikar, and lives on in the people who lead the school today.

Founder

Mrs. Sheela Koppikar

Teaching is my passion, and nurturing young minds fills me with joy. At our school, we are shaping a generation of confident, compassionate, and curious individuals.

Principal

Mrs. Neeta Koppikar

We at SKVM provide a conducive and loving atmosphere to bring out the best in every child. Each child is unique to us.

Chief Academic Officer

Mrs. Unnati Palankar

This school has played a significant role in shaping who I am today. More than anything, I aspire to provide students with the same nurturing upbringing that I experienced here.

The purpose that guides us

Looking ahead, with heart & soul.

Our Vision

To nurture thoughtful, capable and compassionate individuals who learn with curiosity, act with integrity and lead with heart & soul.

An education that helps children discover their potential while recognising their responsibility to the wider world.

Our Mission

To make learning engaging, purposeful and connected to everyday life. To encourage clear thinking, confident expression and a willingness to keep learning. To cultivate kindness, respect and responsibility through daily school experiences. To work with families in supporting each child’s academic, social and personal growth.
