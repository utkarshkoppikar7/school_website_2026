Admissions

Your next chapter, with SKVM.

Choosing a school is a meaningful decision. Start with a conversation, get to know us and find the information your family needs.

A warm welcome to your family

Let’s get to know each other.

Explore the school’s story, discover Life at SKVM and get in touch with the office. The school will confirm grade availability, eligibility and the admission requirements that apply to your child. A first conversation is not a confirmation of admission.

Facilities offered at school

A place for every stage of childhood.

Play Group

Our youngest learners begin with play. Toddlers sing, build, paint and explore in a safe, caring space, and settle into school life at their own pace.

Nursery

A variety of learning activities welcomes children into the world of play-based learning. Stories, rhymes, colours and shapes turn every day into a discovery.

LKG & UKG

The kindergarten years build the foundations for school. Children learn their first letters and numbers, speak up with growing confidence, and learn to work and play together.

Primary Classes

From Class 1 to Class 5, children build strong reading, writing and maths skills, and explore science, languages, art and sport. Competitions, exhibitions and assemblies give every child a chance to take part.

Also at SKVM

Day Care

A safe, caring space for children beyond school hours, so working parents can have peace of mind. Our staff look after every child with the same warmth they bring to the classroom.
