Mental Math Competition

Find the pattern. Enjoy the possibility.

A space for number sense, clear thinking and the satisfaction of finding a way through a problem. Confidence matters as much as pace.

One problem. Different ways.

12 + 8

10 + 2 + 8

10 + 10 = 20

Learning example • not a competition question

The thinking behind the answer

Numbers make sense when ideas connect.

01

Notice relationships

Recognise patterns, compare quantities and use familiar number combinations to approach a new problem.

02

Choose a strategy

Break a calculation into manageable parts and explore an approach that feels clear and reliable.

03

Explain & reflect

Talk through the thinking behind an answer and learn from another student’s method.

A positive way to prepare

Steady practice.
A curious mind.

Use small everyday number puzzles, talk about different methods and celebrate a well-explained idea. Accuracy and understanding should be encouraged before speed. The school will share the approved level of difficulty and event format.
