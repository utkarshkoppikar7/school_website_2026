Events at SKVM

Where participation becomes possibility.

Words, numbers, movement and discovery. Explore six ways for children to express themselves, try a challenge and learn together.

Express & Explore

Monthly Competitions

Regular opportunities to try something new, share an idea and build confidence through participation.
Words & Expression

Spell Bee Competition

A celebration of words that encourages careful listening, vocabulary and the courage to speak.
Numbers & Reasoning

Mental Math Competition

An invitation to think clearly, notice patterns and discover the satisfaction of working with numbers.
Movement & Teamwork

Sports

Space to move, play fairly and learn how individual effort contributes to a team.
Curiosity & Discovery

Science Exhibition

A platform for questions, models and explanations that make scientific thinking visible.

A Saturday space for engaging activities and shared learning. The school will confirm the programme.
