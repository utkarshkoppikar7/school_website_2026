School Assembly

Together, we begin.

A shared moment in the school day. A chance to listen, participate and feel part of something larger than a classroom.

A shared sense of belonging

One school.
Many voices.
A common beginning.

Assembly can be a place where children practise attentive listening, discover their voice and reflect on the values of a school community.

The approach is simple: create meaningful opportunities for participation while encouraging respect for others. The final format, frequency and programme will be confirmed by SKVM.

Assembly elements

Listen. Share. Reflect.

01

Listen with attention

School announcements and shared reflections can help students begin with awareness and purpose.

02

Find a voice

Short student contributions can offer a supported space to practise speaking clearly and confidently.

03

Carry values forward

Thoughtful themes can connect respect, responsibility and kindness with everyday school life.
