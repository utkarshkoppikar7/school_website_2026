Classroom Activities

When ideas come to life.

Making, discussing, discovering. Classroom experiences can help children turn a lesson into something they truly understand.

Learning is an active verb

A question. A shared idea. A small discovery that stays.

Possibilities in the classroom

Many ways to explore one idea.

Make & discover

Connect abstract ideas with things children can see, touch and test.

  • Simple models and demonstrations
  • Observation and recording
  • Creative work with everyday materials

Speak & listen

Build expression while learning to listen carefully to other points of view.

  • Storytelling and reading aloud
  • Thoughtful classroom discussions
  • Sharing ideas with a small group

Think & collaborate

Learn to explain a choice, work together and appreciate more than one way forward.

  • Number and language puzzles
  • Pair and group challenges
  • Reflection on different approaches
After the activity

The learning continues in reflection.

  • What did you notice?
  • How did your group approach the task?
  • What might you try differently next time?

Simple questions help children recognise how they learn, not only what they make.
