Sports at SKVM

Play with spirit. Grow together.

Movement, effort and the joy of being part of a team. Sport offers lessons that can stay with children well beyond the playing field.

More than the final score

Give your best.
Respect the game.
Lift your team.

A positive sporting experience makes room for effort, cooperation and fair play. It can help children enjoy movement and appreciate the part each person plays.

At SKVM, the emphasis is on participation, safe practice and respect for teammates and opponents alike.

Values on the field

What we practise when we play.

01

Participation

Encourage children to take part, try a new challenge and develop a positive relationship with movement.

02

Teamwork

Practise communication, shared responsibility and encouragement, whatever the outcome.

03

Sportsmanship

Respect rules, listen to guidance and recognise the effort of every participant.
