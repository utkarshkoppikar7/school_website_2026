Monthly Competitions

A fresh challenge. A chance to grow.

Regular opportunities to participate can help children discover interests, practise skills and find confidence in trying.

The value is in taking part

An idea worth sharing starts with a little courage.

Monthly competitions can give students a purposeful way to express what they know and explore something unfamiliar. The emphasis in this programme is on preparation, participation and reflection, not simply the final result.

Theme ideas

Different interests. Room for everyone.

01

Language & expression

Themes could include storytelling, reading aloud or written expression, with formats approved by the school.

02

Creative thinking

Making, drawing and other creative prompts can invite children to communicate an idea in their own way.

03

Reasoning & discovery

Age-appropriate puzzles and inquiry tasks can encourage careful observation and thoughtful problem-solving.
